I am an undergraduate in Atmospheric Sciences at Nanjing University, conducting research on heavy precipitation forecasting under the guidance of Prof. Huiling Yuan and her Hydrometeorology Research Group (NJUHML). The group works on hydrometeorological modeling, AI weather models, and deep learning methods.

研究方向包括强降水诊断与预报、生成式降水后处理及预报不确定性表征。目前重点研究级联二分类与扩散模型相结合的降水后处理方法，以及降水可预报性的尺度依赖性与关键大气变量的影响。

My research focuses on the diagnosis and forecasting of heavy precipitation, generative-model-based post-processing, and the representation of forecast uncertainty. I study cascade binary classification with diffusion-based post-processing, as well as the scale dependence of precipitation predictability and the role of atmospheric variables.

Improving Extreme Precipitation Forecasts via Cascade Binary Classification With Diffusion Post-Processing

Dai, L., Yuan, H., Liu, Y., Li, X., Gu, X., Li, J., & Zhao, Y.

Journal of Geophysical Research: Machine Learning and Computation, 3, e2026JH001347.

针对强降水样本稀缺、不同雨强阈值难以兼顾的问题，我们设计了级联二分类（CBC）框架：让 15 个独立二分类模型分别判断降水是否超过特定阈值，再逐级整合预测结果。这种设计减少了多阈值共同训练时的目标冲突，提升了极端降水的识别能力；最终通过扩散后处理则进一步校正降水强度并细化空间结构。

To address the scarcity of heavy-rainfall samples and competing objectives across intensity thresholds, we developed a cascade binary classification (CBC) module. Fifteen independent U-Net classifiers each predict whether precipitation exceeds a specific threshold, and their outputs are combined through hierarchical stacking. This threshold-specific design avoids cross-threshold optimization conflicts and improves extreme precipitation detection. A diffusion-based post-processing module then corrects rainfall biases and refines spatial detail.
